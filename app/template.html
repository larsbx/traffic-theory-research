WHAT IS PROVED HERE

Streets to lanes

STREETS TO LANES

Each map is a 720 × 480 m window of OpenStreetMap, cut into 96 × 64 cells of 7.5 m, one car length. One-way streets become one lane. Two-way streets become two, on the side the country drives on. Lanes are offset by (s/2)(|cos θ|+|sin θ|) so the staircases of diagonal streets cannot interlock. Any loop shorter than 12 cells that is not a real crossing box is a rasterising artefact and is removed. Cars move cell by cell, but each cell is drawn at its point on the real street line, so streets look straight while the dynamics stay on the grid.

The rule: BML on a map

THE RULE: BML ON A MAP

Every move is one cell, horizontal or vertical. Half-steps alternate: horizontal movers, then vertical movers. That is the east-then-north update of Biham, Middleton and Levine (1992), and a two-phase signal at every junction. On a torus whose cars only go east or north, the engine reproduces bml.model.step exactly. The tests replay 80 Python orbits under both rules. CLASSIC moves a car only into a cell that was empty before the half-step. TRAIN lets a queue move together and a full ring rotate, as in Austin–Benjamini.

Traffic laws

TRAFFIC LAWS

Pure BML flips the phase every tick, so both axes fill a crossing and its 2 × 2 box locks. The city adds the laws real cities use. Signals admit a junction only to the axis with green for G half-steps; G = 1 is exactly BML. Don’t-block-the-box lets a car enter a junction only if its whole path through it is clear. Turns across oncoming traffic can be banned. Cars dawdle with probability p, as in Nagel–Schreckenberg.

Why frozen cars are certain

WHY FROZEN CARS ARE CERTAIN

Let β(k) be the car blocking car k. Cars choose their route only after they move, so a cycle of β never moves again under CLASSIC. Under TRAIN the same holds unless the cycle is a straight ring. Every car whose β-path reaches such a cycle is frozen too. This is the map form of Lemma G0. A test checks that no certified car ever moves again, across both rules, every law, dawdling and breakdowns. A jam loop is a directed cycle of lanes, so it needs at least girth cars, the Theorem G1 bound min(W,H) in city form. Grids like Midtown have a girth of 50+ cells, so they almost never lock.

Nucleation and capture

NUCLEATION AND CAPTURE

Following the repository’s freezing note, forming a permanent core and capturing the rest of the traffic are separate events. In a closed city, every car that keeps driving at random eventually reaches a permanent core, so one jam loop takes the whole map. Watch FROZEN climb after a JAM LOOP appears: nucleation is rare and sudden, capture is steady.

Any place: the OSM API

ANY PLACE: THE OSM API

Run npm start in app/ and the simulator is served together with a small OpenStreetMap API: /api/geocode (Nominatim), /api/roads (Overpass, falling back to the OSM API), /api/network (the lane graph: cells, junctions, girth) and /api/simulate (a headless run with a time series and the final jam certificate). LOAD then works for any place on Earth. The server identifies itself to OpenStreetMap, sends Nominatim at most one request a second, and caches every result. This hosted copy cannot make outside requests, so it offers the six built-in places.
