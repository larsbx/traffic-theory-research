# B10.5 — weighted cyclic renewal

Baseline: merged PR #48, `7154c36e11f2c1597838abb16c4806b5fe5dff69`.
This is a proof-bank increment, not a claim of B10.5 closure. Lean build and axiom-audit evidence must be recorded separately.

## Gap semantics and upper bound

A column word has alphabet Cell² (nine ordered pairs). A plug is (N,N).
A gap is read west from its downstream plug. Its mass is the sum of iid
product weights of words with no plugs and with total east count at least the
largest distance of a lone north. No conditioning or normalization occurs.
For u=1-p, v=p/2, the nonplug weights are u², 2uv, v², 2uv, 2v²;
plug weight a=v². G(0)=1, G(1)=1-a-p(1-p). The DP on (east count,
farthest lone) follows by appending a column at distance g+1: a non-lone
adds 0,1,2 east cars leaving the farthest lone unchanged; a lone adds 0 or 1
and resets the distance to g+1. Summing states with east >= distance gives
G(g). All weights remain raw. Multiplying cell weights by 2*den(p) proves
that the integer DP counts the same weights over D^g, D=(2*den(p))².

`runGapCondition` is a geometry-independent predicate on initial states.
If a plug exists, every nonplug column belongs to a unique maximal gap.
A maximal gap is plug-bounded: the only changing membership edges touch
its endpoint plugs. Given a lone in the gap, take its forward run ending
immediately before the downstream plug, of length d. The run condition
then gives d <= eastCountIn(gap). Conversely, if all maximal gaps satisfy
this inequality, any nonempty plug-bounded G containing a lone must contain
its entire maximal gap: along an edge with two nonplugs, membership cannot
change. A run of d nonplugs from the lone cannot exceed its distance to the
next plug. Monotonicity of east counts gives d <= eastCountIn G. The zero
run is vacuous. This proves equivalence to Python's gap_condition.
The new candidate theorem applies the existing B10.3 kernel lemma and
finite union bound; the equivalence to Python still needs mechanization.

## Unique marked-block decomposition

Fix W>0 and the labelled ring Z/WZ. Let a block of length l consist of a
plug followed by l-1 westward nonplug columns satisfying the gap test.
The finite set B_l of blocks has mass b_l=a G(l-1). Let C_m be all lists
of such blocks of total length m; C_0 contains exactly the empty list.
Cut at the first block: for m>0, C_m is the disjoint union over 1<=l<=m
of B_l × C_(m-l). Product weights give c_0=1 and
c_m=sum_l b_l c_(m-l).

For a valid labelled ring, locate the first plug east of or at index 0,
that is, the least j in [0,W-1] at which the column is a plug. Read W
columns west from j, splitting before each plug. This gives a first block
B of length l, followed by an ordered list T of blocks of total length W-l.
The index zero occurs at westward offset j in B, so 0<=j<l. Indeed there
is no plug at indices 0,...,j-1, and the next plug west of j must lie
strictly beyond that offset (or the ring ends).

Conversely, for every l, j in [0,l-1], B in B_l and T in C_(W-l),
concatenate B and T. Write its t-th column at labelled index (j-t) mod W.
The map is a permutation of W indices. There is a plug at index j and
none at indices 0,...,j-1, so encoding selects exactly j. All blocks and
gaps are recovered by splitting at plugs. Conversely decoding a ring's
code restores each column. Thus the two maps are mutual inverses, without
quotienting by rotations; even periodic and all-plug rings have one code.

The column product is invariant under the permutation, and factors as
the product of the first-block and remainder weights. Summing over the
marked offset yields l identical contributions. Therefore
Gamma_W = sum_(l=1)^W l b_l c_(W-l).
For nonnegative weights Gamma_W <= W c_W.
For p in [0,1], Theorem A upper bound is
q_W <= P(Bin(2W,p/2)>=W) + Gamma_W.
The binomial term is obtained by partitioning states by their east-cell
subset: each subset of cardinality k has mass v^k(1-v)^(2W-k).
The proof of the weighted bijection above is mathematical prose; the
Lean candidate proves only the generic weight transport / marked finite
sum, not its concrete geometric instantiation or the binomial identity.

## Theorem P — simpler direct obstruction

The pattern P, L0, P on three consecutive columns, where L0 is either
(N,empty) or (empty,N), makes eventual gridlock impossible. The middle
column has a north car, no east car, and full north columns on both sides.
No east car can enter it, while its north car always has an empty target.
Equivalently apply B10.3 with G the singleton, d=1: 1 <= 0 is impossible.
This needs no bound on total east population. The candidate Lean theorem
`noJam_isolated_north` implements this obstruction via B10.3.

For 0<p<1 the pattern mass is delta=a² p(1-p)>0. Take floor(W/3) disjoint
triples. They are independent. Every eventual jam avoids every one, giving
q_W(p) <= (1-delta)^floor(W/3) for all positive W (for W<3 the bound is 1).
Set rho=1-delta/3 in (0,1). By expansion rho³ >= 1-delta, hence
q_W <= rho^(3 floor(W/3)) <= rho^(-2) rho^W. At p=0 the configuration is
empty, so q=0; at p=1 every occupied configuration is frozen and nonempty.
The real-valued version needs a real-weight extension or a transfer from
rational polynomial identities by continuity. The finite rational triple
independence, bound and endpoints remain to be compiled/mechanized.
This is weaker numerically than the optimized renewal rates but proves
Theorem P without analytic renewal or Chernoff estimates.

  to maximal gaps; construct concrete finite marked-block and first-block
  equivalences, prove factorization of weights and raw DP recurrence;
  formalize binomial tail and exact mirrored orientation (or phase shift).
- B10.6 / Theorem M: check 56 exact finite witnesses (24..79) in Lean;
  prove the uniform W>=80 inequality including the exponential-moment bound,
  rational tail bound, lower estimate and monotone ratio extension. The
  local forbidden triple route does not establish this cutoff.
- B10.7 / Theorem P: use direct triple obstruction, disjoint group weight
  factorization, rational exponential majorant, endpoints and extension
  to real densities. Existing sharper decay-rate certificates can be a
  separate numerical obligation, not needed for the qualitative theorem.
